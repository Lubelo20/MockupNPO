Why we exist

A name that means freedom.

Nkululeko is the isiZulu word for freedom. It's in our name because it's what we want for every young person we meet.

Read our story →

Freedom to stay in class. Freedom to apply for university. Freedom to walk into a room in clean clothes and feel that you belong there.

Too many teenagers have everything it takes to go far, and not enough to get there. An application fee. A pair of school shoes. A box of pads in exam week. Small things that quietly decide big futures.

So we show up with what we can carry. Food parcels for families in a hard season. Clothing passed from one home to another. Comfort boxes for young women on campus. And an afternoon beside a learner, filling in the forms that open the next door.

We registered in 2022 with a vision and a lot of hard work. Every parcel, box and school visit since has been delivered by hand, and accounted for.

“The idea and the vision became a reality. Then the hard work began, and a foundation was laid.”

— Sinalenjabulo Joy Ndlovu, Founder & Director
